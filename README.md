## Favorite Series: Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill

> *"Sometimes the best adventure in another world is just making a really good meal."*

**Campfire Cooking in Another World** (*Tondemo Skill de Isekai Hourou Meshi*) is a chill, slow-life isekai series about an ordinary guy named **Mukohda Tsuyoshi** who gets accidentally summoned to a fantasy world with a seemingly useless skill—*Online Supermarket*.

### Why I Enjoy It
* **Relaxed Pacing:** No world-ending stress—just good food, traveling, and hanging out with companions.
* **Great Cast:** Watching a legendary Fenrir named **Fel** act like a giant food-obsessed dog never gets old.
* **Mouthwatering Animation:** The cooking scenes look insanely detailed.

### Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. **Fel** – The mighty legendary beast who is completely ruled by his stomach.
2. **Sui** – An adorable baby slime with surprising power and an appetite to match.
3. **Mukohda** – The reluctant chef just trying to live a peaceful life.
